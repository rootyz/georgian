Original audio and exact source revision
https://github.com/noBloodOnTheLeaves/alphabet_learner/tree/4d036715350fdeba020bbcd7b9148140d4c0ea0c/public/audio

For this edition the original Ogg Vorbis clips were converted to mono
32 kHz, 80 kbps MP3 and named by modern Georgian alphabet position. Each
original filename is the corresponding Georgian letter followed by .ogg.
