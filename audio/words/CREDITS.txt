Georgian whole-word pronunciation examples

All 33 MP3 files in this directory contain synthesized speech. Each clip reads a word from the original deck in the Georgian voice of eSpeak NG. In the current app, only the “Synthesized guide” buttons under “Another word” use these files. The main “Hear word” buttons use the recordings credited in ../SPEAKERS.txt.

The rendering voice uses glottal-release models for the ejective series. The initial sound in ჭიქა uses the postalveolar ejective affricate model. Each example was rendered as one word, volume-matched and encoded as a mono MP3. The built-in voice produces approximate pronunciation and has a computer-generated timbre.

Speech engine: eSpeak NG contributors, https://github.com/espeak-ng/espeak-ng
Render wrapper: text2wav.node.js by abbr, https://github.com/abbr/text2wav.node.js/tree/f4e9dae9dfc7c63ba7f9a229480653c156053628
